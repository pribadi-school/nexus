# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

School IT staff install and pair the player on corridor Android TVs. Day-to-day
playback should run without someone manually opening the app each morning.

## Product Purpose

Play Nexus-managed MP4 playlists from downloaded files, display online website
items, and return to playback after TV startup or standby.

## Operating Context

Management and original files remain on Nexus's PHP/cPanel web app. The native
player has a remote-friendly server/pairing flow and a small controls overlay.
Automatic startup requires one-time Home-app configuration on the TV; device
manufacturer restrictions may require administrator setup.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Android 7.1 or newer, TV remote or touch navigation, English interface.
- Native portrait and landscape display coordinates, including 4K output.
- Optional device-owner provisioning enables persistent Home and lock task mode.
- Code pairing approved in the signed-in Nexus web app.
- Retain the last fully downloaded playlist during connectivity loss.
- Websites need connectivity; original videos use the cPanel hosting plan.
- Preserve the Nexus emblem and colors; use readable native TV controls.
- The app must not bypass Android startup, certificate or device restrictions.
